## Open decisions found later in this hunt (not changed, they are design)
5. **Any member with write may answer an ask that was addressed to someone else, and that takes the ask off the addressee's list.** `answerAssistance` lets a writer answer any ask in the case (a thread reply; only a read-only member or an outsider has to be the addressee), and `pending` hides every ask that has any answer. So a member with write can answer an agent's open asks with anything (found by giving a read-and-write attacker every op): the asker sees the answer, the agent that was asked never sees the question. Options: (a) leave it, the asker re-asks if the answer is wrong; (b) keep the ask in the addressee's list until the addressee or the asker has answered; (c) only the addressee (and deciders) may answer. I would pick (b): it keeps the thread-reply behaviour and does not let a stranger close someone else's question.
6. **The Host retries a throwing step for ever; there is no dead-letter rule.** See "Poison events" under Checked and clean. A deterministic harness bug on one event stalls that agent in that case until the harness is fixed. Skip after N tries and report, or keep the stall visible?
7. **A decision can have up to 100,000 options** (only the argument size limits it) and every inbox of the people who may decide lists them all (now bounded by characters, not by count of options). A separate limit such as 50 options would match how decisions are used.
8. **For the other session, in the Desk files it has not committed yet (read only, nothing changed there).**
