# routing-story, 2026-10-10

104 invented chat messages (a week at a small Finnish software company, 5 people, 13 destinations: 5 realms and 8 channels incl. a casual `ch-random`). Generated by the assistant, so it says nothing about real traffic. Shadow mode.

**Label correction, disclosed:** banter and office messages were first labelled "should stay unrouted", although a casual-chat channel exists. The first Jev run showed the mistake; I relabelled 13 messages to `ch-random` and re-ran. The numbers below are with the corrected labels (the labels were changed after seeing results, so treat them as less clean than a blind set).

| classifier | correct of 104 |
|---|---|
| keyword baseline | 18 |
| Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`, 3 runs | 61 / 60 / 62 |

By message kind (Jev, 3 runs): clear 23/29, english 4/4, banter 9/11, terse 4/6, ack 5/8, ambiguous 3/8, **followup 5-6/23**, noaccess 3/5, overlap 1/3, office 0/2.

Routed vs threshold (run 1): at minConfidence 0.0 78 routed, 51 right (65%); 0.5: 74%; 0.7: 81% (52 routed, 42 right); 0.9: 90% (39 routed, 35 right, coverage 38%). On realistic chat, unlike the injection set, a higher confidence does mean fewer wrong routes, but even 0.9 is below the 95% target and coverage falls to a third.

## What it shows
1. **Replies are the big hole.** 23 of the 104 messages are thread replies ("joo voin", "katon iltapäivällä", "kyllä, levy täynnä"). The classifier sees one message, so it gets 5 or 6 of 23. Giving it the previous message of the thread, or the channel the sender just wrote in, is the obvious next idea; nothing in the router passes that today.
2. **It answers with the realm when unsure.** 14 answers were a realm (mostly `realm-eng` for design talk, `realm-infra` for incident follow-ups) instead of the specific channel. A realm is a legal address, so Core would accept it, but it is a less useful place. Open question: count a realm as acceptable, or never offer realms when channels exist.
3. **Channels with close topics confuse it.** Design questions about the deploy pipeline went to `ch-deploys` (4 times); a thread about a slow report went to `ch-deploys` too.
4. **Chat noise gets routed.** "parane pian!", "joo!", "hahaha" went to `ch-random` at 0.4-1.0. Whether that is wrong depends on whether acknowledgements should be posted at all.
5. **No access is not always respected by text:** "Prod is down, who is on call?" from someone who cannot reach incidents went to `realm-eng` (0.96). It is a place that person can write to, so Core allows it, but it is the wrong place for the message.

Run-to-run: totals moved by 2 (60-62); the answer to "needs-context" flipped once.
